## Story

Pas To Kima is a seaside restaurant in Argaka, Cyprus. We cook simple Mediterranean food with Cypriot soul: good olive oil, fresh herbs, charcoal-grilled meats and fish, vibrant salads, and generous platters.

Every Thursday we host a Traditional Greek Night with live singers and dancers; come for the food, stay for the kefi!

Small, friendly, and right by the wave. Come hungry, leave happy.

> “Good food, good mood, always by the wave.” — The Pas To Kima Family

At a glance:

- Every Thursday: Traditional Greek Night (live singers & dancers)
- Charcoal grill, seafood, salads, burgers
- Seaside, relaxed and family-friendly
- Vegetarian options available
- Christenings and weddings

## What we believe

- Philoxenia: Real hospitality; everyone at our tables is a guest of honor.
- From-scratch cooking: Dips, dressings and sauces made fresh daily.
- Local first: We prioritise seasonal produce and Cypriot makers whenever possible.
- Charcoal and flame: Simple grilling that lets quality speak for itself.
- Honest portions: Generous plates designed for sharing.
- Consistency: The same care at lunchtime or late into the evening.
